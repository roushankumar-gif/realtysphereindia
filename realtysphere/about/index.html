About RealtySphere

RealtySphere provides independent real estate information, property research, location guides and home-buying education for readers in India, starting with Sector 16, Noida.

We are not a real estate broker, property developer or RERA-registered business, and we do not represent any builder. We do not sell property or earn commission from listings.

How we work
