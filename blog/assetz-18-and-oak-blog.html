About Assetz 18 & Oak

Assetz 18 & Oak is presented by Assetz Property Group as a luxury plotted development spread across 177 acres. The project is located at Sevaganapalli Village and Post, Hosur Taluk, Krishnagiri District, Tamil Nadu, PIN 635103. The official project website highlights landscaped open spaces, a lake and a golf-oriented lifestyle.

The master-plan page lists 246 luxury villas, while another official Assetz page describes the development as a plotted project. Because project descriptions and inventory counts can vary by phase or page, prospective buyers should request the latest approved master plan, plot/villa inventory and applicable phase documents before making a booking decision.

The project’s headline lifestyle feature is an 18-hole, Par 71 golf course measuring more than 6,500 yards and occupying approximately 107 acres, according to the developer. Do not assume every plot or home has an unobstructed golf-course view: confirm the exact location, orientation and view corridor of the specific inventory being considered.

Developer Overview and Buyer Due Diligence

Assetz Property Group is the developer associated with 18 & Oak. Review the company’s official project materials, but do not rely on brand reputation alone when assessing a property. RERA and legal checks

The project website displays RERA registration number TN/30/BUILDING/0426/2022. Before paying a booking amount, look up the registration and relevant phase on the official Tamil Nadu Real Estate Regulatory Authority website or confirm the correct official portal with the developer. Match the registered project name, promoter, land details, approvals and completion information to the documents you receive.

  • Ask for the current RERA registration certificate and applicable phase details.
  • Review the approved layout/master plan and the exact plot or villa number.
  • Have an independent property lawyer review title documents, encumbrances and the sale agreement.
  • Confirm whether the transaction is for a plot, a completed villa or a villa to be constructed, and understand what is included.
  • Check the payment schedule, cancellation terms, taxes, registration costs and other charges.
  • Obtain written clarification of possession, infrastructure completion and maintenance obligations.

Investment Considerations: Potential and Risks

A golf-oriented setting, a large land parcel and substantial open space may appeal to buyers seeking a lifestyle property. The project’s location near the Hosur–Attibele–Sarjapur belt may also be relevant to people working across the wider regional employment market. These characteristics, however, do not establish that a property will appreciate or deliver a particular rental return.

Factors to assess

  • Personal use: Consider whether the location, travel time and facilities fit your day-to-day needs.
  • Total acquisition cost: Include the property price, applicable taxes, registration, legal review, interiors or construction, maintenance and any club or membership charges.
  • Liquidity: Large-ticket villas and plots can take time to resell. Research actual comparable transactions where reliable data is available.
  • Delivery and execution: Verify current construction or infrastructure status, phase-specific commitments and applicable approvals.
  • Market risk: Demand, interest rates, competing supply and broader economic conditions can change.
  • Golf-related costs: Confirm access rules, membership fees, course operations and any restrictions on views or use.

Do not treat projected appreciation, future infrastructure or promotional claims as guaranteed outcomes. Compare the property with alternatives using the same assumptions and a realistic holding period.
